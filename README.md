# Hi there, I'm Luisa! 👋

I'm a Computer Science student at UFMG (Brazil) passionate about the entire data lifecycle. I find purpose in everything from raw data transformation to advanced analytics and Data Science, building AI applications that turn complex datasets into real-world value. 

Currently, I work as a **Junior Data Engineer at KIS Solutions** and serve as a **Data Science Mentor** in the UFMG + Samarco residency program. My work bridges the gap between robust Data Engineering and cutting-edge AI, with a special interest in Data Science and AI-driven products.

* **AI Agents:** Exploring strategic implementation of autonomous agents for automation and content workflows.
* **Creator Intelligence Studio:** Building a Full Stack SaaS for creators to unify analytics with AI-driven insights.
* **Applied Research:** I've had a long journey in Deep Learning, from basics to SOTA models. I worked with Computer Vision for Petrobras for 2.5 years and in MIR (Musical Information Retrieval) with Thiago Poppe.
